UX/UI Designer

I'm a UX/UI and graphic designer, driven by turning complex problems into design that's simple and genuinely usable — covering everything from research, wireframes and prototyping through to visual and motion design.

I'm a Bangkok-based product designer with 6+ years of experience designing digital products across fintech, health and commerce. I care deeply about clarity, craft, and the small details that make an interface feel effortless.
